## What changes the price

Four things move the number:

- **Array size and layout.** More panels and more edge to seal means more mesh and more time.
- **Roof access and pitch.** A steep or hard-to-reach roof takes longer and needs more safety setup.
- **Existing nesting and droppings.** Nests and droppings have to be removed and the area sanitized before anything is sealed, so a longer-running problem takes more work.
- **Whether you bundle cleaning with proofing.** Doing both in one visit saves a second trip.

## How mesh compares with other methods on cost

Mesh is the only method that physically blocks the space under the array. Spikes and sonic deterrents can push pigeons to a neighboring roof instead of solving the problem, so the gap under your panels stays open and you may end up paying again later. Decoys tend to stop working once pigeons get used to them. A loose or gapped DIY install often just relocates the problem to the next unsealed edge, and redoing it professionally after a failed attempt usually costs more than starting with a pro.
