Pigeons don't peck through solar glass, but the gap between a raised panel and the roof is exactly the kind of sheltered spot they look for in the Arizona heat — and once they've nested there, the real damage isn't from the birds themselves.

## What actually causes the damage

Droppings accumulate on the roof and gutters, nesting material can block drainage, and the panels above the nest run hotter and dirtier than the rest of the array — all of which chips away at output over time.

## The fix

A properly fitted stainless mesh, sealed to the mounting rails rather than draped over the panels, keeps birds out without affecting airflow or your manufacturer's warranty.
